Know what you're
signing on Sui

SuiShield simulates every transaction against live chain state and shows you what leaves your wallet, what arrives, and whether anything looks wrong.

Transaction preview

Dry-run every transaction before signing. See coins in and out, objects moved, contracts called.

Risk detection

Flags known drainer patterns, abnormal outflows, and unverified contracts before your wallet sees the request.

Phishing warnings

Connected sites checked against the Sui Guardians blocklist on every page load. Warns before you connect.

How it works

1

dApp requests a signature

You interact with any Sui dApp — swap, stake, mint, whatever.

2

SuiShield intercepts

Before your wallet sees it, the transaction is simulated using Sui's native simulateTransaction.

3

You see what happens

Balance changes, object mutations, contract calls — broken down so you understand what you're approving.

4

Approve or reject

Looks right — approve, goes to your wallet. Looks wrong — reject, nothing was sent.
