What it catches

Blind signatures

Every transaction gets dry-run first. You see coins in and out, objects transferred, contracts called — not raw bytes.

Drainer contracts

Known attack patterns flagged before your wallet ever sees the request. Abnormal outflows to unverified addresses get a warning.

Phishing sites

Connected sites checked against the Sui Guardians blocklist on page load. You get warned before you connect, not after.

How it works

1

dApp asks for a signature

You swap, stake, mint — anything that needs signing.

2

SuiShield intercepts

The request gets simulated using Sui's simulateTransaction before your wallet sees it.

3

You see what happens

Balance changes, object moves, contract calls — plain language, not hex.

4

You decide

Approve and it goes to your wallet. Reject and nothing was sent.
