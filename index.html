See what a transaction does before you sign it

SuiShield sits between the dApp and your wallet. Every signing request gets simulated against live chain state — you see what moves, then you decide.

What it catches

Blind signatures

Every transaction gets dry-run first. You see coins in and out, objects transferred, contracts called.

Drainer contracts

Known attack patterns flagged before your wallet ever sees the request. Abnormal outflows get a warning.

Phishing sites

Connected sites checked against the Sui Guardians blocklist. Warns before you connect, not after.
